# Contributing

krikri-playbook is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later (see LICENSE). By
contributing you agree your contribution is licensed under the same terms.

krikri aims to match Ansible's behavior and output exactly. Ansible, ansible-lint
and yamllint are GPLv3+, so consulting their source for parity work is
acceptable. Keep comments factual, don't claim a clean-room origin for this
codebase, and prefer describing observed behavior over pasting large upstream
blocks. See NOTICE for provenance.
